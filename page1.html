Overview

The Centre of Excellence in Smart Manufacturing & EVT was established in 2024 as part of the Government of India’s push for Indigenous Technology Development. The CoE was set up with support from Siemens and Wipro, with Wipro serving as an implementation partner. The goal was to create a national-level research facility that integrates academia and industry, focusing on Industry 4.0, automation, and sustainable mobility. Within a short span, the CoE has built advanced research labs for students, researchers, and industry professionals as a platform for the integration between them for skill development and research initiatives.

Vision

1. To become a leading research hub in Smart Manufacturing & EVT.

2. To contribute towards India's sustainable and digital transformation.

3. To bridge the gap between academia and industry through innovation and skill development.

4. To establish global collaborations in Industry 4.0 and advanced mobility solutions.

5. To expand research into emerging domains such as autonomous systems, AI-driven robotics, and green energy solutions.

6. In 5 years, the CoE aims to be a global leader in advanced manufacturing and electric mobility research, fostering international partnerships and industry-driven innovation. 

Mission

1. To drive technological innovation in Smart Manufacturing and Electric Vehicle domains.

2. To create a collaborative platform for academia, industry, and government agencies.

3. To support India's transition to Industry 4.0 and green mobility through R&D and workforce training.

4. To develop competency in solving real-world interdisciplinary problems through research-driven projects.

5. To provide research and consultancy services for industries, enabling the adoption of cutting-edge technologies.

Objectives

Education & Training
Offer certified training programs for students, faculty, and industry professionals in emerging technologies.

Research & Development
Advance research in factory automation, IIoT, electric mobility, and digital manufacturing, with a focus on indigenous technology development.

Industry Collaboration
Partner with leading industries to foster innovation, technology transfer, and adoption of Industry 4.0 solutions in Indian industries.

Entrepreneurship Support
Incubate startups and provide consulting services in smart manufacturing and EV technology.

Sustainability
Develop energy-efficient solutions for manufacturing and electric vehicles, promoting green and sustainable industrial practices.
