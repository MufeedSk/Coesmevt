About CoE

The Centre of Excellence (CoE) in Smart Manufacturing (SM) and Electric Vehicle Technology (EVT) at IIT Tirupati is a premier research and innovation hub focused on advancing next-generation manufacturing and sustainable mobility. Established in 2024, the CoE aims to facilitate cutting-edge research, industry collaboration, and skill development in these transformative domains. With state-of-the-art laboratories and partnerships with leading industries, the CoE aligns with India’s national initiatives for technology development. The CoE also supports thrust areas of IIT Tirupati, including Smart Manufacturing, Energy, and Infrastructure, which contribute to India's vision for self-reliant technology growth.

Director's Message

At IIT Tirupati, we are committed to fostering innovation and excellence in engineering education and research. The establishment of the Centre of Excellence in Smart Manufacturing & Electric Vehicle Technologies is a testament to our dedication to advancing India's technological capabilities. This initiative aims to bridge the gap between academia and industry, promoting interdisciplinary collaboration and driving sustainable solutions for the future.

Head's Message

The Centre of Excellence in Smart Manufacturing & Electric Vehicle Technologies at IIT Tirupati serves as a dynamic platform for research, innovation, and skill development. Our focus is on developing indigenous technologies that align with the principles of Industry 4.0 and sustainable mobility. Through collaborative efforts with industry partners and academic institutions, we aim to nurture talent and drive technological advancements that contribute to India's growth in the manufacturing and electric vehicle sectors.

CoE Laboratories

Digital Manufacturing Robotics and Automation Lab

The Digital Manufacturing & Robotics Lab at IIT Tirupati's Centre of Excellence focuses on robotic automation, digital twin simulation, and manufacturing process optimization. This lab is designed to support advanced manufacturing techniques, including virtual process validation, robotics programming, and ergonomic checks. It provides a platform for academic research, industry collaboration, and skill development in smart manufacturing.

Electric and Autonomous Vehicle Lab

The Electric & Autonomous Vehicle Laboratory (EAVL) focuses on the development and testing of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS) technologies. ADAS encompasses a suite of intelligent systems designed to improve vehicle safety, enhance driving comfort, and support more efficient transportation.

Factory Automation Lab

The FA Lab is designed to provide practical exposure to industrial automation using Programmable Logic Controllers. In this lab, students learn how to program, troubleshoot, and interface PLCS with input and output devices such as sensors, motors, relays, and actuators. The lab helps develop skills in ladder logic programming, real-time process control, and system diagnostics.

Industrial Internet Of Things Lab

IIoT stands for the Industrial Internet of Things. It refers to using Internet of Things (IoT) technologies and applications in industrial settings. The IIoT connects industrial devices, machines, sensors, and other equipment to the internet to gather and exchange data. This connectivity allows for increased automation, improved efficiency, and enhanced data analytics in industrial processes.

Microgrid Lab

The Microgrid Lab at IIT Tirupati focuses on renewable energy integration, smart grid management, and energy storage systems. It provides an advanced platform for real-time grid monitoring, EV-grid interaction, and blackout recovery solutions.

Product Design Lab

The Product Design Lab at IIT Tirupati's Centre of Excellence focuses on digital -design, simulation, and manufacturing. This lab supports the complete product development lifecycle, from conceptualization to final design for production. It enables students and professionals to work on innovative product designs and simulate advanced manufacturing techniques.

Simulation and Test Lab Hardware

The Simulation & Test Lab (Hardware) at IIT Tirupati's Centre of Excellence provides a high-performance platform for real-time data acquisition, vibration testing, and system diagnostics. This lab is designed to help students and researchers perform hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing, ensuring accurate analysis of dynamic and acoustic systems. The facility includes industry-grade sensors, actuators, and signal processing tools to deliver precise mechanical and acoustic assessments.

Simulation and Test Lab Software

The Simulation & Test Lab (Software) at IIT Tirupati's Centre of Excellence specializes in multiphysics simulation and real-world system analysis. This lab enables advanced computational modeling for fluid dynamics, heat transfer, structural mechanics, electromagnetics, and acoustics. The facility provides students and researchers with tools to predict system performance and optimize engineering designs.
