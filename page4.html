Digital Manufacturing Robotics and Automation Lab

The Digital Manufacturing & Robotics Lab at IIT Tirupati's Centre of Excellence focuses on robotic automation, digital twin simulation, and manufacturing process optimization. This lab is designed to support advanced manufacturing techniques, including virtual process validation, robotics programming, and ergonomic checks. It provides a platform for academic research, industry collaboration, and skill development in smart manufacturing.

Digital Manufacturing Robotics & Automation Lab

Key Focus Areas

1. Design and optimize robotic work-cells and factory layouts
2. Plan and simulate robotic paths for safe and efficient operation
3. Integrate robots into automated manufacturing workflows
4. Simulate assembly and virtual commissioning of processes
5. Monitor quality and optimize operations using data-driven tools

Software / Hardware

1. Tecnomatix enables 3D simulation for optimizing manufacturing processes and assembly lines.
2. Teamcenter integrates design, development, and production with a digital twin approach.
3. Opcenter optimizes production planning, quality management, and supply chain.
4. Opcenter APS optimizes production schedules and resource allocation.
5. Opcenter Execution and Intelligence provides real-time monitoring and performance analytics for manufacturing.

Training Programs

1. Hands-on training in robotic automation, digital manufacturing, and industrial simulation for engineering students.
2. Advanced courses in robotic cell design, automation programming, and factory optimization for industry professionals.
3. Research and development focusing on additive manufacturing, robotic cell development, and human-robot collaboration.

Potential Beneficiaries

1. Automotive and manufacturing industries requiring robotic automation and process optimization.
2. Startups and R&D labs focusing on smart automation and digital manufacturing.
3. Industries seeking cost-effective and intelligent robotic solutions.
