Simulation and Test Lab - Software

The Simulation & Test Lab (Software) at IIT Tirupati's Centre of Excellence specializes in multiphysics simulation and real-world system analysis. This lab enables advanced computational modeling for fluid dynamics, heat transfer, structural mechanics, electromagnetics, and acoustics. The facility provides students and researchers with tools to predict system performance and optimize engineering designs.

Simulation and Test Lab - Software

Key Focus Areas

1. Fluid Dynamics: Analysis of flow behavior and aerodynamics for industrial applications.
2. Heat Transfer: Simulation of thermal management systems and heat exchange processes.
3. Hybrid Multiphase Analysis: Modeling fluid-solid interactions in manufacturing and energy sectors.
4. Electromagnetics: Study of electromagnetic interference and wireless communication systems.
5. Acoustics & Noise Control: Simulation of vibration and noise propagation in mechanical systems.
6. Aeroelastic analysis: Flutter, divergence and trim analyses.
7. Reacting flows and electrochemistry 

Software / Hardware

1. STAR-CCM+: A multiphysics computational tool for simulating fluid flow, heat transfer, and structural interactions.
2. Simcenter 3D Academic Bundle: Provides an integrated environment for physics-based simulation and design optimization.
3. Simcenter Amesim: Used for multi-domain system simulation, enabling engineers to predict system behavior under various operating conditions.
4. Menter-Mechanical & Electrical Design: Software tools for finite element analysis (FEA), structural design, and electrical wiring simulations.
5. HEEDS Academic Bundle: Optimizes product design performance through automated design exploration.
6. gROMS Software Academic Bundle
7. Menter-Vehicle Networking Design

Training Programs

1. For Engineering Students: Hands-on training on computational fluid dynamics (CFD), structural simulations, and system-level modeling.
2. For Industry Professionals: Courses on simulation-driven design, digital twin modeling, and predictive system analysis.
3. Internship and Faculty Development Programs: Focused on simulation applications in automotive, aerospace, and energy sectors. 

Potential Beneficiaries

1. Researchers working on fluid dynamics, acoustics, and system optimization.
2. Industries focused on aerospace, automotive, energy, and electronic systems development.
3. Startups developing smart manufacturing and AI-driven simulation solutions.
